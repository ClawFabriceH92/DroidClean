The Battery card now measures real battery life: time left before the next
recharge, average life of a full charge, and how often you recharge. Android
provides none of these — the app derives them from a reading history that stays
on the device.

Periods where the phone was powered off, rebooted, or recharged unobserved are
discarded: they would skew the averages.

Expect a few charge cycles before the estimates appear.
